Before the first cut
  • Measure your actual plywood thickness. If it is not what this sheet says, tell whoever made the design file and regenerate: the inside sizes stay fixed and the outside sizes move.
  • Caliper the screen's picture area before cutting the window (C3). The window size here is provisional; the pocket (C4) is from the maker's published module size.
  • Hole sizes in inches are rounded up to the next 1/32". Use the metric bit if you have it.
  • Check the thread length of the buttons and the volume knob's bushing against the 18 mm front. Where a thread is too short for its nut, counterbore from the back.
  • Speaker screw holes are marked from the driver's own template, not from this sheet.
  • Rips are spaced for a 1/8" [3.2] blade. Rip a hair fat and trim to the number.
